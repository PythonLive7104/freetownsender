===============================================================================
PART 1  -  WHAT THIS APP DOES
===============================================================================

EndTime watches your email accounts and answers messages for you.

You tell it two things:

  1. WHAT TO LOOK FOR  - for example, any email with "Invoice" in the
                         subject line.

  2. WHAT TO SEND BACK - the message that should be sent in reply.

From then on it checks your inbox around the clock. When an email arrives that
matches, it sends your reply automatically. You do not have to be logged in,
and your computer does not have to be switched on.

One person can manage many email accounts this way, from one screen.

Here is the whole idea in a picture:

        Someone emails you
                |
                v
        EndTime checks your inbox every few seconds
                |
                v
        Does the subject line match one of your rules?
                |
         +------+------+
         |             |
        YES            NO
         |             |
         v             v
    Sends your      Does nothing.
    reply back      The email just sits
    automatically   in your inbox as normal.

IMPORTANT: EndTime never deletes, moves, or changes your emails. It only
reads them and sends replies. Anything it does not have a rule for is left
completely alone.


===============================================================================
PART 2  -  SETTING UP, STEP BY STEP
===============================================================================

There are three things to set up, and they must be done in this order,
because each one builds on the last:

        STEP 1              STEP 2              STEP 3
     Add your inbox  -->  Write a reply  -->  Make a rule
                                              (joins them together)

Do not skip ahead. You cannot create a rule until you have a reply for it to
send, and a reply is no use until there is an inbox to send it from.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 1  -  ADD YOUR EMAIL ACCOUNT
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Go to "Mailbox" in the left-hand menu, then click "Add mailbox".

You will be asked for:

  Display name      A name just for you, so you can tell your accounts
                    apart. For example: "Sales inbox". Nobody else sees it.

  Email address     The account you want answered.

  Password          NOT your normal email password. Almost every provider
                    needs a separate "app password" instead - see the box
                    below before you fill this in.

  Provider          Pick your email service (Gmail, Outlook, and so on) and
                    the technical boxes below fill themselves in.

The remaining boxes - IMAP host, IMAP port, SMTP host, SMTP port - are filled
in for you when you choose a provider. Leave them alone unless your email
provider has told you to use something different.

Two optional settings worth knowing:

  Also scan Spam / Junk    Turn this on if genuine messages sometimes end up
                           in your spam folder. EndTime will then check there
                           too, so those get answered as well.

  Poll every (seconds)     How often this inbox is checked. Leave it blank to
                           use the standard setting.

>> BEFORE YOU MOVE ON: click "Test".

   This checks the address and password actually work. If it fails now, no
   replies will ever be sent from this account, and nothing else you set up
   will help. Fix it here first.

HOW TO GET AN APP PASSWORD
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

GMAIL / GOOGLE WORKSPACE

  Create it here:
      https://myaccount.google.com/apppasswords

  Step-by-step help from Google:
      https://support.google.com/accounts/answer/185833

  Turn on 2-Step Verification first, if you have not already:
      https://myaccount.google.com/signinoptions/two-step-verification

  Google shows you a 16-character code with spaces, like "abcd efgh ijkl
  mnop". You can type it with or without the spaces. Copy it immediately -
  Google will not show it to you again.


YAHOO MAIL

  Create it here:
      https://login.yahoo.com/account/security

  Choose "Generate app password" or "Manage app passwords".

  Step-by-step help from Yahoo:
      https://help.yahoo.com/kb/SLN15241.html

  Note: Yahoo limits how much mail you can send in a short period. Keep your
  volumes modest or it will temporarily block sending.


ICLOUD MAIL

  Create it here:
      https://appleid.apple.com

  Go to "Sign-In & Security", then "App-Specific Passwords".

  Step-by-step help from Apple:
      https://support.apple.com/en-us/102654


ZOHO MAIL

  Create it here:
      https://accounts.zoho.com/home#security/apppasswords

  On free Zoho plans you may also have to switch IMAP on before anything
  works. That setting is in Zoho Mail's own settings, under Mail Accounts.


OUTLOOK / HOTMAIL / MICROSOFT 365  -  PLEASE READ

  ** Microsoft is switching this off. **

  Microsoft is withdrawing the ability for apps to sign in with a password
  at all, in favour of a newer method that this app does not yet support.
  On newer Microsoft accounts, sign-in may fail no matter what password you
  use, including a correctly generated app password. This is a limitation on
  Microsoft's side, not a fault in your setup.

  If you have an older account, an app password may still work:
      https://account.microsoft.com/security

  If it does not work, your options are to use a different email provider
  for automatic replies, or to ask whoever administers your system whether
  support has been added since.


OTHER PROVIDERS

  Look in your email account's own settings for "Security", then "App
  passwords" or "Application-specific passwords". If your provider has no
  such feature, your ordinary password will usually work - but you may need
  to switch on "IMAP access" in their settings first.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

WHY THIS IS SAFER, NOT RISKIER

An app password only works for this one app. It cannot be used to sign in to
your email on the web, it cannot change your account settings, and you can
revoke it at any time from the same page you created it on - without
touching your real password or disturbing anything else you use.

WHAT HAPPENS TO IT HERE

Whatever you enter is scrambled before it is stored, using a method that
cannot be reversed. Nobody can read it back out of the system - not other
users, not an administrator, not even you. If you ever need to change it,
you simply type the new one in.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 2  -  WRITE THE REPLY
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Go to "Auto-reply" in the left-hand menu, then click "New template".

  Template name     A name just for you, so you can find it later. For
                    example: "Invoice acknowledgement".

  Subject           The subject line of the reply that gets sent.

  Body              The message itself.

You can write a plain message and stop there. But you can also drop in tags
that fill themselves in with real details - the sender's name, today's date,
and so on. Those are covered in PART 3, and they are what makes an automatic
reply feel personal instead of robotic.

>> BEFORE YOU MOVE ON: click "Preview".

   This shows you exactly what somebody receives, with all the tags filled
   in with sample details. Always preview before you rely on a template.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 3  -  MAKE THE RULE
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

This is the step that switches everything on. A rule joins your inbox to your
reply and says when to use it.

Go to "Rules" in the left-hand menu, then click "New rule".

  Name              A name just for you.

  Match type        How to compare the subject line. You have four choices:

                      Subject contains      The word appears anywhere in the
                                            subject. This is the one you
                                            want almost every time.

                      Subject equals        The subject must match exactly,
                                            word for word.

                      Subject starts with   The subject must begin with it.

                      Subject matches regex Advanced pattern matching. Ignore
                                            this unless you already know what
                                            it means.

  Match value       The word or phrase to look for. For example: Invoice

  Case sensitive    Leave this OFF and "invoice", "Invoice" and "INVOICE"
                    are all treated the same. This is usually what you want.

  Reply template    Which of your replies to send. This is the one you
                    wrote in Step 2.

  Mailboxes         Which inboxes this rule applies to. Leave it empty and
                    it applies to all of them.

  Attachments       Any files to send along with the reply (optional).

  Priority          A number. Lower numbers are checked first. The default
                    is 100.

>> HOW RULES ARE CHOSEN - THIS TRIPS PEOPLE UP

   Rules are checked in order, and THE FIRST ONE THAT MATCHES WINS. Once a
   rule matches, the ones below it are never even looked at.

   So put your most specific rules first, and general catch-all rules last.

   For example, if you have these two rules:

       Priority 100:  subject contains "Invoice"        -> General reply
       Priority 200:  subject contains "Invoice overdue" -> Urgent reply

   ...then an email titled "Invoice overdue" gets the GENERAL reply, because
   the first rule matched and stopped the search. To fix it, give the more
   specific rule a lower number so it is checked first:

       Priority 50:   subject contains "Invoice overdue" -> Urgent reply
       Priority 100:  subject contains "Invoice"         -> General reply

There is a test box on the Rules page. Type in a subject line and it tells
you which rule would fire. Use it - it takes two seconds and saves confusion.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
THAT IS IT
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Your automatic replies are now running. Send yourself a test email with a
matching subject line and watch it come back.

Do not panic if the reply is not instant. There is a deliberate short delay
so replies do not look machine-generated. See "Reply delay" in PART 4.


===============================================================================
PART 3  -  WRITING REPLIES THAT FILL THEMSELVES IN
===============================================================================

A tag is a short piece of text in double curly brackets, like this:

    {{sender_name}}

When the reply is sent, the tag disappears and real information takes its
place. So if you write this:

    {{greeting}} {{sender_first_name}},

    Thank you for your message on {{received_date}}. We will get back to
    you by {{business_day_plus_2}}.

    Your reference is {{ticket_id}}.

...somebody called Jane Doe receives this:

    Good morning Jane,

    Thank you for your message on Wednesday, August 26, 2026. We will get
    back to you by Friday, August 28, 2026.

    Your reference is 68D34BCA.

YOU DO NOT NEED TO TYPE TAGS BY HAND. Underneath the message box there is a
row of tags you can click, and the tag is inserted wherever your cursor is.
Hover over any of them and a short note explains what it does.

Here is everything available.


ABOUT THE PERSON WHO WROTE IN
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  {{sender_name}}          Their full name              Jane Doe
  {{sender_first_name}}    Just their first name        Jane
  {{sender_last_name}}     Just their surname           Doe
  {{sender_email}}         Their email address          jane@example.com
  {{sender_user}}          The part before the @        jane
  {{sender_domain}}        The part after the @         example.com


ABOUT THEIR MESSAGE
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  {{original_subject}}     Their subject line, exactly as sent
  {{subject_clean}}        The same, with "Re:" and "Fwd:" removed
  {{quoted_body}}          Their message, with > in front of each line,
                           the way a normal email reply quotes
  {{original_body}}        Their message with no quote marks
  {{ticket_id}}            A short reference code, e.g. 68D34BCA
  {{received_date}}        The day they wrote
  {{received_time}}        The time they wrote
  {{message_id}}           A technical identifier. You will rarely need it.


ABOUT YOU
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  {{mailbox_name}}         The name of the inbox replying
  {{mailbox_email}}        The address the reply is sent from
  {{mailbox_domain}}       Your own domain
  {{workspace_name}}       Your workspace name
  {{rule_name}}            The rule that triggered this reply
  {{template_name}}        The name of the reply being sent


DATE AND TIME
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  {{greeting}}             Good morning / Good afternoon / Good evening,
                           chosen automatically from the time of day
  {{date}}                 Wednesday, August 26, 2026
  {{date_short}}           2026-08-26
  {{date_us}}              08/26/2026
  {{date_eu}}              26/08/2026
  {{time}}                 8:03 PM
  {{time_24}}              20:03
  {{datetime}}             The date and time together
  {{day_name}}             Wednesday
  {{month_name}}           August
  {{year}}                 2026
  {{timezone}}             UTC


PROMISING A DEADLINE
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
You can change the number in any of these.

  {{date_plus_3}}          Three days from today
  {{date_minus_1}}         Yesterday
  {{business_day_plus_2}}  Two WORKING days from today. Weekends are
                           skipped, so from a Friday this lands on Tuesday,
                           not Sunday. Use this one for "we will reply
                           within X working days".


RANDOM CODES
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Each of these is different in every single email sent.

  {{ran_letter_10}}        10 random letters
  {{ran_digit_6}}          6 random digits
  {{ran_alnum_12}}         12 random letters and digits
  {{ran_hex_8}}            8 random characters, 0-9 and a-f
  {{uuid}}                 A unique code that never repeats


YOUR OWN TAGS
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
You can invent your own on the "Placeholders" page - see PART 4. Useful for
anything you repeat often, like your phone number or office address.

IF YOU MISTYPE A TAG, nothing breaks. It simply appears in the sent email
exactly as you typed it, brackets and all. This is another reason to always
use Preview before relying on a template.


===============================================================================
PART 4  -  EVERY PAGE, EXPLAINED
===============================================================================

The menu on the left is grouped into four sections.

Every page also has a short note at the top explaining what it is for. You
can hide it with the "Hide" button, and bring it back with "Show tips". To
bring back all of them at once, go to Settings and click "Show again" next
to "Page tips".


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
MAIN
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

DASHBOARD
  Your home screen. Shows how many replies have gone out, how many are
  waiting to send, and what has come in. Start here to confirm at a glance
  that everything is ticking over.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
AUTOMATION  -  the parts that do the actual work
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

MAILBOX
  Your email accounts. Add, edit, and test them here. Covered in PART 2.

AUTO-REPLY
  Your reply templates - the messages that get sent. Covered in PART 2.

RULES
  The instructions that decide which reply is sent when. Covered in PART 2.
  This is the page that actually switches your automation on.

CONFIGURATION
  Three settings that apply to everything:

    Reply delay (minutes)     How long to wait before answering. A short
                              delay makes replies feel human rather than
                              machine-generated.

    Poll interval (seconds)   How often your inboxes are checked. Shorter
                              means faster replies but more work for your
                              email provider. The standard setting is fine
                              for almost everyone.

    Signature                 Text added to the bottom of EVERY automatic
                              reply. Put your company name and contact
                              details here once, instead of in every
                              template.

  The starting values are sensible. Only change them if you have a reason.

LISTENERS
  A live view of what is happening right now - which inboxes are being
  checked, when each was last checked, and what has just arrived. This is
  the page to open when you want to see the system working, or to work out
  why it is not.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
TOOLS  -  optional extras
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

PLACEHOLDERS
  Your own reusable snippets. Create one called "phone" holding your phone
  number, and you can drop {{phone}} into any reply. Change it here later
  and every reply that uses it updates automatically - you do not have to
  edit each template.

  You give each one a Key (the short name you type, letters and numbers
  only), a Label, an optional Description, and the Static value - the text
  it stands for.

LINKS
  Short web links that count clicks. Put one in a reply and this page shows
  you how many people opened it. Useful for measuring whether anyone acts on
  your automatic replies.

  You give it a Name, a Slug (the short bit that appears in the web address),
  and the Destination URL - the real page people should land on.

ATTACHMENTS
  Files you can send with a reply, such as a brochure or price list. Upload
  once here, then attach it to a rule so it goes out with that rule's reply.

PROXIES
  Advanced and optional. Sends your mail out through a different internet
  connection, which some providers require when sending in large volumes.

  LEAVE THIS PAGE COMPLETELY EMPTY unless somebody has specifically given
  you proxy details to enter. An empty Proxies page is normal and correct.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SYSTEM  -  your account and access
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

TEAM
  Everything you set up lives in a "workspace". Invite colleagues here and
  they share the same inboxes, replies, and rules.

  Invite by username or email address. There are three roles:

    Owner     Full control, including billing. There is one owner.
    Admin     Can manage everything and invite others.
    Member    Can use the system day to day.

  If you belong to more than one workspace, switch between them using the
  menu at the top right of the screen. Check which workspace you are in
  before making changes - it is easy to edit the wrong one.

SUBSCRIPTION
  Your plan and payments. Payment is made to the wallet address shown, then
  you paste the transaction reference so it can be matched to your account.
  Your plan activates once the payment is confirmed.

SECURITY
  Change your password here. Below that is a log of important activity on
  your account, such as sign-ins. Worth glancing at occasionally.

TELEGRAM
  Optional. Get a message on your phone via Telegram when mail arrives or a
  reply is sent, so you do not have to keep this page open.

  Setting it up takes two pieces of information:
    - A bot token, which you get from @BotFather in Telegram
    - Your Chat ID, which you get from @userinfobot in Telegram

  Paste both in and click "Send test" to confirm it reaches you.

SETTINGS
  Your name and email address, switching between light and dark appearance,
  replaying the setup guide, and bringing back hidden page tips.


===============================================================================
PART 5  -  CHECKING IT IS WORKING
===============================================================================

THE ONE-MINUTE TEST

  1. Send yourself an email with a subject line that matches one of your
     rules.
  2. Open the "Listeners" page and watch it arrive.
  3. Wait for your reply delay to pass (see Configuration).
  4. Check the inbox you sent from. The reply should be there.

WHAT TO GLANCE AT NOW AND THEN

  Dashboard    Are the "sent" numbers going up as you would expect?
  Listeners    Are your inboxes still being checked regularly?
  Links        Is anyone clicking your tracked links?

If the numbers on the Dashboard stop moving entirely for a day or more,
something has stopped. See PART 6.


===============================================================================
PART 6  -  WHEN SOMETHING GOES WRONG
===============================================================================

NO REPLIES ARE BEING SENT AT ALL

  Work through this in order. Nine times out of ten it is one of the first
  three.

  1. Is the rule switched on?
     Open Rules and check the rule is marked active.

  2. Is the template switched on?
     Open Auto-reply and check the template is marked active.

  3. Does the subject line actually match?
     Use the test box on the Rules page. Type the exact subject and see
     whether it fires. Watch for extra spaces and different spellings.

  4. Is the mailbox connection still good?
     Open Mailbox and click "Test". Passwords get changed and expire.

  5. Is the inbox still being checked?
     Open Listeners. If it has not been checked for a long time, contact
     whoever administers your system - a background service may have
     stopped.


THE WRONG REPLY IS BEING SENT

  Almost always the rule ordering. Remember: the first matching rule wins
  and the rest are ignored. Give your more specific rule a LOWER priority
  number so it gets checked first. See PART 2, Step 3.


REPLIES ARE SLOW

  Check "Reply delay" on the Configuration page - a delay is deliberate and
  normal. Also check "Poll interval", which controls how often the inbox is
  looked at. A reply cannot arrive faster than the next check.


A TAG APPEARED IN THE EMAIL AS {{something}}

  The tag name was mistyped, so there was nothing to substitute. Open the
  template, delete it, and click the tag from the row beneath the message
  box instead of typing it. Then use Preview to confirm.


"TEST" FAILS ON A MAILBOX

  This is the single most common problem, and the cause is nearly always
  the password.

  1. Are you using an app password?
     Your normal email password will be refused by almost every provider.
     You need a separate app password. Full instructions and links for each
     provider are in PART 2, Step 1.

  2. Did you switch on two-step verification first?
     Most providers will not even offer you the app password option until
     two-step verification is enabled on your account.

  3. Did you copy the whole code?
     Google's codes are 16 characters and are shown with spaces. Missing a
     character is easy to do. Generate a fresh one and paste it rather than
     typing it.

  4. Is it a Microsoft account?
     Outlook, Hotmail, and Microsoft 365 may fail no matter what you enter.
     Microsoft is withdrawing password sign-in for apps, and this app cannot
     use the replacement method yet. See PART 2, Step 1.

  5. Check the email address itself for typos.

  6. If you edited the IMAP or SMTP boxes by hand, re-select your Provider
     to reset them to the correct values.


I HID A PAGE NOTE AND WANT IT BACK

  Click "Show tips" at the top of that page. To restore all of them at once,
  go to Settings and click "Show again" beside "Page tips".


SOMETHING ELSE

  Take a screenshot, note what you were doing, and send it to whoever
  administers your system. The Security page's activity log and the
  Listeners page are usually where the answer is.


===============================================================================
PART 7  -  PLAIN-ENGLISH GLOSSARY
===============================================================================

  APP PASSWORD    A separate password you generate inside your own email
                  account, for one specific program to use. It works only
                  for that program, cannot be used to sign in on the web,
                  and can be cancelled at any time without changing your
                  real password. Nearly every provider now requires one.
                  Free, and takes about a minute. Links for each provider
                  are in PART 2, Step 1.

  TWO-STEP        An extra sign-in check on your email account, usually a
  VERIFICATION    code sent to your phone. Also called 2FA. Most providers
                  will not let you create an app password until this is
                  switched on.

  ATTACHMENT      A file sent along with an email.

  IMAP            The technical method used to READ your incoming email.
                  Filled in automatically when you pick your provider.

  MAILBOX         One email account that the app watches.

  PLACEHOLDER     Your own reusable snippet of text, used through a tag.

  POLL / POLLING  Checking an inbox for new mail. "Poll interval" is how
                  often that happens.

  PRIORITY        A number on a rule deciding the order rules are checked.
                  Lower numbers are checked first.

  RULE            The instruction linking "what to look for" to "what to
                  send back".

  SIGNATURE       Text added to the bottom of every automatic reply.

  SLUG            The short readable part at the end of a web address.

  SMTP            The technical method used to SEND email. Filled in
                  automatically when you pick your provider.

  TAG             Text in double curly brackets, like {{sender_name}}, that
                  is replaced with real information when the reply is sent.

  TEMPLATE        A saved reply message.

  WORKSPACE       A shared space holding your inboxes, replies, and rules.
                  Everyone invited to it sees the same things.
